# Free Nurse CE Kit

Free printable continuing-education helpers for registered nurses: a CE tracker, a license-renewal calendar, a course log, and reflection templates. Fillable, printable, large-print friendly, and no account needed. Education only — not medical advice, and not a substitute for your state board of nursing requirements.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). Dylan Asdale is a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC. His book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## What's inside

| File | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| `ce-tracker.html` / `ce-tracker.md` | Log CE courses and activities, track hours by category, and total progress toward renewal. |
| `license-renewal-calendar.html` / `license-renewal-calendar.md` | 12-month renewal checklist with fillable task boxes and large-print friendly dates. |
| `course-log.html` / `course-log.md` | Per-course detail log: provider, date, hours, cost, certificate number, and application notes. |
| `reflection-templates.html` / `reflection-templates.md` | Guided reflection prompts for each completed course (what you learned, how you'll apply it, open questions). |

Every page is a single self-contained HTML file. Open it in any browser, fill it in on screen, and print. No login, no account, no internet needed after download.

## Large-print friendly

All pages use a large base font (18 px for writing space, 16 px+ body text), high contrast, generous line height, and wide ruled areas designed to be printed and filled in by hand. Print styles strip headers/controls so only the worksheet prints.

## How to use

1. Download or clone this folder.
2. Open any `.html` file in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. Fill in the fields on screen, or print blank and write by hand.
4. Print with your browser's print dialog. Background graphics are suppressed for clean output.
5. Keep the completed sheets with your licensure records. Verify all CE requirements with your state board of nursing — requirements vary by state and these templates are not a compliance guarantee.

## Education only

These templates are for continuing-education recordkeeping only. They do not constitute medical advice, nursing advice, or licensure guidance. Always confirm CE hour categories, renewal deadlines, and documentation rules with your state board of nursing and employer.
