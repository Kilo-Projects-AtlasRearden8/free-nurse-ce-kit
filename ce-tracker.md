CE hour categories, renewal deadlines, and documentation rules vary by state — always confirm requirements with your state board of nursing and employer.

## Instructions

1. Enter each completed CE course or activity in the table as you finish it.
2. Record the category your state board assigns (for example: pharmacology, patient safety, general, specialty).
3. Keep certificates in a folder and mark "Y" once the certificate is filed.
4. Total your hours before renewal and confirm you meet your state's requirements.
