CE Reflection Templates

Education only. These templates are for personal continuing-education reflection and recordkeeping. They are not medical advice, nursing advice, or licensure guidance. Reflection is optional and is not a substitute for your state board of nursing requirements.

How to use

Print one copy of the per-course template for each completed CE course. The quarterly and annual templates help you spot patterns before renewal. Fill in on screen or by hand, then file with your course log and certificates.

Template 1 — Per-course reflection (print one per course)

1. What did I learn? (key takeaways)

2. What will I change in my practice?

3. What questions do I still have?

4. Would I recommend this course to a coworker? Why or why not?

Template 2 — Quarterly CE reflection

1. Which course was most useful this quarter, and why?

2. What did I apply at work already?

3. What is still on my to-learn list?

4. Am I on track to meet my renewal CE requirement?

5. What will I do next quarter?

Template 3 — Annual / renewal-cycle reflection

1. What was the most valuable thing I learned this cycle?

2. How did my CE choices affect my practice or patients?

3. Which providers or formats worked best for me?

4. What would I do differently next cycle?

5. Notes for my next renewal (deadlines, categories, providers to try)

Print one copy of Template 1 per course. Large-print friendly: 16 pt+ body text and wide ruled answer areas.
